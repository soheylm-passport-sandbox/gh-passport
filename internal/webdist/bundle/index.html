    IT & research computing / course 2.1.2

    Follow one lesson.
    Verify the result.

    The IT Passport is the lab's guided course for setting up tools and practising safe research-computing tasks. Research computing means the software, storage, and shared computers used to produce research. The Passport selects the lessons needed for your work, remembers where you stopped on this computer, and checks each submitted exercise.

    How the Passport works

    Complete one step
    at a time.

    The browser shows your current lesson and remembers where you stopped.

    1. 01
      Start your local passport

      The beginner start guide explains the required applications, lets you practise one harmless command, then creates or reuses your course folder and lesson list.

    2. 02
      Complete one lesson

      Each command tells you which computer and application to use, such as PowerShell on Windows or Terminal on macOS and Linux. The start guide explains how to open it and run your first command.

    3. 03
      Check and submit the result

      The page checks your work before submission. GitHub records who submitted it, and the automatic check reports any failed requirement.

    4. 04
      Resume from your progress page

      After setup, the launcher reopens your current lesson from any folder. A failed or unavailable check never becomes a pass.
