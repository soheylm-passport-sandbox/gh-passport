    How the Passport works

    Complete one step
    at a time.

    The browser shows your current mission and remembers where you stopped.

    1. 01
      Start your local passport

      One command creates or reuses your local route and personal public exercise record.

    2. 02
      Complete one mission

      Copy only commands labelled for your machine and shell.

    3. 03
      Verify and submit evidence

      GitHub preserves attribution; the controller reports objective failures.

    4. 04
      Resume from the dashboard

      Run gh passport open to return to your current mission. A failed or unavailable check never becomes a pass.
