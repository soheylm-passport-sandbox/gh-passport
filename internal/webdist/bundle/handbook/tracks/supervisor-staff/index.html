    Supervisor And Staff Track

    View this page on GitHubReturn to my progress

    This track covers the decisions that training alone cannot make: who owns the project information, which systems a person may access, where data belongs, and who receives the work when someone leaves.

    Complete this track if you sponsor access, supervise researchers, maintain lab systems, or approve exceptions to normal onboarding policy.

    This page summarizes the track. Follow the current mission in your local Passport; do not use the links below as a second checklist.

    Prerequisites#

    • IT and Research Safety Core
    • Familiarity with the tracks used by people you supervise

    Competencies#

    You can:

    • assign an information owner and classify project data;
    • confirm GitHub, NAS, Euler, software, and guest-account requirements;
    • select the required passport endorsements for each project;
    • review unusual compute, storage, permission, and AI-tool requests;
    • distinguish ETH-wide rules from IDEAL Lab policy;
    • respond to credential, confidentiality, data-loss, and resource incidents;
    • plan handover and revoke unnecessary access at project completion;
    • maintain volatile documentation using primary sources and review dates.

    Mission Coverage#

    1. Lab IT policy
    2. Data and AI policy
    3. IDEAL Lab Euler share policy
    4. Assessment rubric
    5. Content ownership

    Evidence#

    • One completed onboarding packet.
    • A documented decision on storage and AI-tool use.
    • Review of the researcher's assigned route and any separately flagged operational access or ownership decision.
    • An offboarding or handover owner.

    Pass Criteria#

    The researcher can begin without guessing about access, storage, data classification, AI tools, or compute policy. Exceptions are written, scoped, time-limited, and owned.
