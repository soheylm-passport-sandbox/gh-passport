    Important Corrections To Older Copies#

    • A minimal CPU job does not request 64 CPUs and 250 GB RAM.
    • The public share is CPU-only; changing only the account on a GPU job is invalid.
    • Current ETH guidance says not to specify a partition unless the workload truly depends on one.
    • Current scratch capacity is 2.5 TB per user and files are purged after about two weeks; scratch is not backed up.
    • Long or unattended work belongs in sbatch, not a persistent GPU tunnel.
